    Concept · AI-built

    Letting support teams trust an AI agent with refunds

    An agent finds a double charge, cites the policy it used, and stops for a person before it moves money or emails a customer.

    Cesar Navarro · cnavarro.business@gmail.com

    Problem
    Support teams want agents to handle refunds, but one wrong refund costs money and trust. Most agent tools show a chat log, not what the agent is about to do.
    User
    A support lead who reviews agent actions between tickets and owns the refund policy.
    Decisions
    1. The run is a pipeline with people as gates. Work visibly stops at each decision, so nobody wonders what happens next.
    2. Approve the exact call, not a summary. The approval shows the tool, the charge and the amount, and the amount can be changed.
    3. Every claim cites a source. The billing record and the policy line sit next to the decision.
    4. Nothing leaves without a person. The refund and the customer reply each have their own gate. Drafts say "not sent."
    5. Failures say what did not happen. "Nothing was changed" and "Not sent. Draft saved."
    Outcome
    An AI-built concept of the full run: ticket, tool calls, approval, refund, reply and send, with empty, loading, tool error and send error states. Not yet tested with real users or a real billing system.
    Label
    Concept. The company, customer and data are fictional. The interface and its state flow are real and run in the browser.
