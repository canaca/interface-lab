    Concept · prototype

    Explaining why monthly revenue moved

    When MRR drops, a finance or success lead needs the cause in minutes, not a spreadsheet afternoon. The month breaks into its parts, then into the accounts behind them.

    Cesar Navarro · cnavarro.business@gmail.com

    Role
    Concept direction
    Tools
    AI-assisted build (Claude, Cursor)
    Problem
    Dashboards show that revenue fell, but not why. Teams export data and rebuild the story by hand, so action comes late.
    User
    A head of customer success at a SaaS company, preparing for the monthly revenue review.
    Decisions
    1. The title is the finding. Every view states what happened, with the number.
    2. The month splits into a bridge. The drop breaks into new, expansion, contraction and churn, so the biggest cause is obvious.
    3. Drill from cause to customer. One click from the churn step to the accounts, and one more to the warning signs they showed.
    4. Honest about the data. If billing data is incomplete, the view says so before anyone acts on it.
    5. End in an action. The explanation turns into a win-back task with an owner, confirmed before it's created.
    Outcome
    A clickable prototype of the full path, from the drop to the bridge, the accounts, the signals and the task, with empty, loading, incomplete-data and task-error states. Not yet tested with real users or real billing data.
    Label
    Concept. The company, accounts and numbers are fictional. The interface and its state flow are real and run in the browser.
