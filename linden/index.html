Morning book —

    Client card select one

    Case study

    The morning list should say why, not only who.

    A store manager opens the book and sees who is due, what they asked for last time, and a note the advisor can send by hand.

    Role
    Concept direction
    Tools
    AI-assisted build (Claude, Cursor)
    Problem
    Follow-ups live in memory and in a group chat. The person on the floor does not know the size, the occasion, or why today is the day.
    User
    A client advisor or store manager at a boutique. Not a finance analyst, and not a media buyer.
    Decisions
    The queue is open follow-ups, with a reason on the card. Preferences can be added and removed. A message is a draft: the page says nothing is sent. Moving a client to another advisor asks first. A failed save keeps the note. Export is the filtered sample.
    Outcome
    A working prototype of the book, the card, the draft, undo, and export, with empty, loading, and save-error states. Not tested with a real store team. No sales figures.
    Date
    October 2026 concept, directed by Cesar Navarro. AI-assisted build (Claude, Cursor). Not a client engagement. Linden draws on his jewelry clienteling as Vice President at Talisman Collection Fine Jewelers in El Dorado Hills (May 2022 – Mar 2024), and on his GIA Applied Jewelry Professional Diploma (2023) and GIA Diamond Grading Certificate (in-person at AGS Laboratories, 2022).
    Label
    Concept. Linden, the advisors, and the clients are fictional.
