Concept · October 2026

Seeing which accounts need a person this week

A success or account manager opens one book, sees why an account is at risk, and logs the next step. The score is the sum of the signals on the card.

Cesar Navarro · cnavarro.business@gmail.com

Problem
Renewal week arrives and the book is a spreadsheet of names. The person who owns the relationship cannot see, in one place, who went quiet, whose champion left, and whose onboarding never finished.
User
A customer success or account manager working a book of business-to-business accounts. Not a finance analyst, and not a reporting-tool specialist.
Decisions
  1. The score shows its work. Five signals, each with points. No hidden model and no revenue forecast.
  2. Filters change the book. Health, owner, plan, and a focus (needs a touch, renewal soon, onboarding stalled) all narrow the same list. The counts above are those filters.
  3. A next step is confirmed. Check-in, save plan, and onboarding follow-up ask for a note before anything is logged. Snooze is explicit.
  4. A failed save keeps the note. The error says nothing was logged. Try again uses the same words.
  5. Export is the filtered sample. The file says it is fictional. It is not a live book.
Outcome
A clickable prototype of the book, the account, the task list, undo, and export, with empty, loading, and save-error states. Not tested with a real success team. No churn rate is claimed.
Date
October 2026 concept, directed by Cesar Navarro with AI tools (Claude, Cursor). Not a client engagement, and not a finance tool.
Label
