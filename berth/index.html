FIELDNOTE · SAMPLE BOOK Concept · Oct 2026

Sample onboarding book for Fieldnote, a fictional product. Progress is steps done. A nudge is a task on this page. Nothing is emailed.

New accounts —

    Checklist select one

    Case study

    A new account should show the stuck step, and who owes it.

    The success manager sees five steps, a blocker written in words, and a nudge that stays on the page.

    Role
    Concept direction
    Tools
    AI-assisted build (Claude, Cursor)
    Problem
    Onboarding is a checklist in a doc. The owner cannot see which step is blocked, or whether the next move is theirs or the client's.
    User
    A customer success manager running the first weeks of a new account. Not a finance analyst.
    Decisions
    Progress is steps marked done, not a score. Marking a later step first asks, and still allows it with a note. A blocker needs a reason. A nudge creates a task. It does not email anyone. Moving the account asks first. A failed save keeps the note.
    Outcome
    A working prototype of the book, the checklist, the nudge list, undo, and export, with empty, loading, and save-error states. Not tested with a real success team. No time-to-value claim.
    Date
    October 2026 concept, directed by Cesar Navarro with AI tools (Claude, Cursor). Not a client engagement. Fieldnote is the same fictional company used in Keel. This is a separate prototype.
    Label
    Concept. Fieldnote, the accounts, and the people are fictional.
