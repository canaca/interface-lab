Concept · October 2026

Showing the follow-up before anything goes out

A local service business already has the lead. Relay is the path after the click or the missed call: an instant reply, a text-back, a stage, and a task. The owner reads the preview first.

Cesar Navarro · cnavarro.business@gmail.com

Problem
The form sits there, or the call hits voicemail, and nobody has a next step. Turning on an automation without seeing the message is how the wrong text goes out.
User
The owner or office manager of a local service business that already runs ads. They are not a CRM administrator. They need to see what would be sent, to whom, and what happens if a step is off.
Decisions
  1. Preview, then nothing sends. Every message card says so. The test run repeats it.
  2. Each trigger can be off. Form, missed call, and stage change are separate switches. An off trigger stops that path.
  3. A duplicate does not get a second message. The sample book includes one, and the run stops there.
  4. The pipeline is the same list. Move a lead with a button or by dragging. Undo puts it back.
  5. A failed preview names what did not happen. Nothing is marked sent, and the stage does not move.
Outcome
A clickable prototype of the builder, the filters, the pipeline, the test run, and the export, with empty, loading, and preview-error states. Not tested with a real owner. No claim that replies got faster.
Date
October 2026 concept, directed by Cesar Navarro with AI tools (Claude, Cursor). The pattern is the kind of follow-up used in a CRM. It is not a GoHighLevel screenshot, not a certification, and not a client account.
Label
Concept. Cedar Heating and every lead are fictional. No prices. Nothing is sent.
