Concept · prototype

Reaching new Meta leads while they're still interested

A new lead gets a text in under a minute, answers two or three questions, and lands on the calendar. The owner just gets the alert.

Cesar Navarro · cnavarro.business@gmail.com

Role
Concept direction
Tools
AI-assisted build (Claude, Cursor)
Problem
Med spas and home-service shops pay for Meta leads, then often reply hours later. By then the lead may have booked with whoever texted first.
Workflow
A lead form sends a text within 60 seconds. An AI asks two or three questions, books a consult, and alerts the owner. If the lead doesn't reply, follow-ups go out at 5 minutes, 1 hour, and the next day. Replying STOP ends everything.
Decisions
  1. The whole process in one place. Every branch, including opt-out and no reply, is a visible step, not a hidden setting.
  2. Texting rules are built in. A2P 10DLC status, the opt-out line, and quiet hours are checked as you edit. A flow that breaks them can't be tested.
  3. English and Spanish side by side. Each text has both versions, with a live count of message segments, since accented letters can change the count.
  4. The AI knows when to hand off to a person. Questions about price, medical concerns, and upset messages go to a person.
What I'd test
Whether owners trust the AI to book without checking first, whether two questions book more than three, and which first text gets more replies in each language.
Goals (not results)
Goals: median first text under 60 seconds, 30% or more of leads booked, and opt-outs under 3%.
Label
Concept. The business, leads and results are fictional demo data. The builder, test run and states are real and run in the browser.
