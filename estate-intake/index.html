Concept · November 2026

A jeweler should see the guess, and should be the one who accepts it

Estate pieces arrive as photos and a story. I designed a sheet where a scripted draft separates what a drawing shows from what it does not, and nothing is marked reviewed until a person says so.

Cesar Navarro · cnavarro.business@gmail.com

Role
Concept direction
Tools
AI-assisted build (Claude, Cursor)
Problem
A counter intake is a stack of photos and a few sentences. A draft that names the stone, or offers a number, gets read as an appraisal. The jeweler then has to undo a conclusion the customer already saw.
User
A jeweler at an independent bench, reviewing a draft before anyone relies on it. The customer questions are what that jeweler would ask A. Rivera, a fictional customer.
Constraints
The page is a static file. It cannot accept a photo, weigh a piece, or look inside a stone. The drawings are samples. It was not tested with jewelers. It must not show a price, a grade, or a stone name.
Decisions
  1. The draft is written ahead of time for these drawings. The page does not pretend to see a picture. Each row says what the drawing shows and how sure that note is, in words.
  2. The center stone is not named. A guess of "diamond" would be the conclusion this page refuses to make.
  3. A person marks it reviewed. The jeweler checks a line that says these are notes, not an appraisal. Undo clears that mark.
  4. Customer questions can be English or Spanish. The rest of the page, including the role and tools lines, stays in English. The questions can grow. They are not cut off.
Rejected
  1. A chat reply only. A paragraph hides which sentence is a guess. Separate rows keep the note next to the reason.
  2. A value, even as a range. Any number would be read as an appraisal. There is no number on the sheet.
  3. Sending the draft to the customer automatically. The jeweler reviews it first. Copy and export stay on this page. Nothing is emailed.
What changed in the design
The first pass of this concept wanted the draft to name the center stone. That name is the mistake. The sheet now says the drawing is not enough, and lists what it could not check. This was not a test with jewelers. What I would ask a jeweler: whether they trust a draft that refuses to name the stone.
Outcome
A working sample of the sheet, the review, undo, copy, and export, with empty, loading, and missing-drawing screens. Not a live service. The described POST /intake-draft route still does not run.
Label
Concept. Fictional bench, customer, and ring. Not an appraisal. No prices.
