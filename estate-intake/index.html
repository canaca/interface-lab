Estate Jewelry Intake Assistant

This is NOT an appraisal. Concept / early access, not a live service.

An idea for independent jewelers. A customer would upload photos of jewelry they already own. An AI would draft an intake sheet. A credentialed jeweler would review that draft.

The sheet would list what the piece appears to be, which metal stamps can be read, a guess at how many stones there are and what they might be, notes on condition, and questions to ask the customer. Those are notes for the jeweler, not a value and not a grade.

How it would work

  1. A customer shares photos of a piece of estate jewelry.
  2. The draft names the item type only as it appears, the stamps that can actually be read, stone count and type as guesses, and what looks worn or damaged.
  3. It also lists questions the jeweler still needs the customer to answer.
  4. A credentialed jeweler reviews the draft before anyone relies on it.

Those steps are the idea. None of them run here. This page does not look at photos.

What this page is

A static description, so a person or an agent can find the idea and read it. GitHub Pages only serves files. It does not accept photos, identify a stone, or say what a piece is worth.

There are no customers and no results to report, because the service is not running. The only next step is the waitlist.
